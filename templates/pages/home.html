EduTrack — College Student Management & Academic Progress System

A centralized higher-education platform designed to maintain an immutable student identity across all academic years, isolate departmental records, track multi-year progress, and deliver actionable academic support.

Core Principle

One permanent student identity across all academic terms, with access strictly controlled by institutional role and departmental scope.

Security & Identity

Custom User & 8 Institutional Roles

Configured at the root before initial migrations: Super Administrator, College Administrator, Principal, HOD, Faculty, Class Advisor, Student, and Guardian.

Data Integrity

Historical Record Preservation

Designed so advancing to subsequent academic years never overwrites historical assessment marks, attendance records, or previous semester outcomes.

Deployment Readiness

SQLite Local • PostgreSQL Production

Environment variables govern configuration cleanly. Standard Django settings structure ready for institutional server deployment.
